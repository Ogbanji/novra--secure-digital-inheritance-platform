  cd .. && npm run dev

================================================================================
ELEVATOR PITCH (100 words)
================================================================================

Novra is a secure digital inheritance platform for the crypto era. Users store 
digital assets (crypto, NFTs, documents, memories) in an end-to-end encrypted 
vault protected by military-grade AES-256 and Shamir's Secret Sharing. A 
dead-man switch auto-triggers after inactivity, releasing assets to heirs with 
full privacy. AI generates legal wills, blockchain notarizes inheritance 
agreements, and multi-signature approval prevents fraud. It's the world's first 
truly decentralized platform where users—not companies—control inheritance.
